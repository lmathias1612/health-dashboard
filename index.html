Privacy Policy

Autonomic Health Dashboard

Last updated: March 2026

What This App Does

The Autonomic Health Dashboard is a personal health monitoring tool that connects to your Whoop wearable to visualize biometric trends and help you identify recovery patterns. It is designed for individual, personal use.

Data We Access

When you connect your Whoop account, the app requests read-only access to:

  • Recovery data (HRV, resting heart rate, SpO2, skin temperature)
  • Sleep data (sleep stages, respiratory rate, sleep performance)
  • Workout data (strain scores, activity type and duration)
  • Basic profile information (for personalization only)

You may also manually enter additional data such as symptom check-ins, subjective wellness scores, and blood test results. This data is entered voluntarily at your discretion.

How Your Data Is Stored

All health data is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

The app uses your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) to keep your data on your machine. Your Whoop API credentials are stored in a local environment file and are never embedded in client-side code or transmitted elsewhere.

Data We Do NOT Collect

This application does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Analytics, usage tracking, or telemetry of any kind
  • Advertising identifiers or marketing data
  • Personal information beyond what you explicitly enter
  • Location data, contacts, or device information

Third-Party Services

The app connects to the Whoop API (api.prod.whoop.com) solely to retrieve your biometric data. This connection is governed by Whoop's own privacy policy. No other third-party services receive your data.

If future integrations are added (such as Google Health Connect or Apple HealthKit), this policy will be updated before any new data connections are established.

Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Export all your data as CSV or JSON at any time via the app's settings
  • Delete all stored data by clearing your browser's local storage or using the in-app reset function
  • Revoke Whoop API access at any time through your Whoop account settings
  • The app functions with whatever scope of data you choose to provide

Data Security

All communication with the Whoop API occurs over HTTPS. Since data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, disk encryption, browser security). We recommend keeping your device and browser up to date.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data handling practices change — particularly if a server-side component or additional integrations are introduced — this policy will be updated with a new revision date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
